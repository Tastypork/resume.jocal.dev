About Me

Welcome to my page! My name is Joshua O'Callaghan, and my journey has been a remarkable one—filled with trials, triumphs, and a steadfast faith that guides me through it all. In May 2020, I survived a 70 miles-per-hour motorcycle accident, a life-altering moment that forever changed my perspective. While the accident left me in a wheelchair, it was also the moment I experienced the grace of Jesus Christ in a profound way. I now live each day with a deep sense of purpose, firmly believing that God has promised me the strength and ability to walk again. My favorite verse, Philippians 4:13, reminds me daily: "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me." My faith isn't just a part of my life; it is the foundation on which everything else is built.

I graduated in 2020 from Arizona State University with a Master's Degree in Computer Science, specializing in Big Data. My time at ASU was transformative, both academically and personally. I discovered a passion for software engineering and problem-solving. Now I work full time as a Software Engineer.

Beyond my professional life, I have a deep passion for a variety of hobbies that allow me to channel my creativity and love for hands-on work. Among these hobbies, I like cars, woodworking, jewelry & watch making, and building computers. Photography is another love of mine, allowing me to capture and preserve the beauty of God's creation in everyday moments. There's something special about freezing time in a photograph, reflecting the world around us, and seeing the divine in the details.

Writing, too, holds a special place in my life. It gives me a voice to express thoughts, reflect on my journey, and share lessons learned along the way. I enjoy writing about a wide range of topics, from personal growth to technical explorations in software development and even theology. It's through writing that I find clarity and connection, both with myself and others who may read my work.

I come from a culturally rich background, with a Jamaican mother and an Irish father, and this has greatly influenced the way I approach life and relationships. I have a deep love for community and believe that we are called to serve and uplift those around us. Meeting new people, hearing their stories, and forming genuine connections brings me joy, whether the conversation centers on theology, life challenges, or technical discussions. I believe that every conversation is an opportunity to learn something new or share something valuable.

My love for the Word of God has been a source of strength and guidance in my life. For those wondering I read primarily AMP! I enjoy dissecting scripture, understanding its context, and applying its teachings to my everyday life. If you're passionate about theology or enjoy deep discussions about faith, I'd love to connect and dive into those topics with you!

In addition to my love for cars, photography, writing, and theology, I am always eager to learn new skills and challenge myself in different areas. Whether it's tinkering with technology, building computers, or exploring a new craft, I find joy in discovering and mastering new things. My journey has been shaped by both the mountains and valleys I've walked through, and I approach life with gratitude, resilience, and an unwavering belief in what lies ahead.

Thank you for taking the time to learn more about me. I hope this gives you a sense of who I am, both as a person and as a believer. Feel free to explore more of my work or reach out—whether you'd like to talk cars, theology, or just share your story, I'm always open for a meaningful conversation. God bless!
