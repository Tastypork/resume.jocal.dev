Who Am I?

Thanks for taking the time to visit my page! I'm Joshua O'Callaghan, a graduate student pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Science with a concentration in Data Science. I have many interests in the realm of Computer Science and I am looking forward to sharing these with you. I graduated with my Computer Science undergraduate degree December of 2019 and I plan on completing my graduate program in December of 2020.

Through completing my undergraduate degree, I have aquired many useful skills that allow me to be a competitive and invaluable engineer for any full-time role. Among these skills, learned both in and outside of the classroom, I have experience in these languages: Python, Java, C++, C, HTML, Javascript, SQL, R, Scheme, Prolog. One of the best skills I have aquired from my collegiate experience is the ability to adapt and learn new things quickly. Combined with my love of learning new things I am ready to suceed wherever I end up!

Outside my degree interests I am a member of the National Society of Black Engineers, volunteer occassionally at Lost Our Home Pet Shelter, and enjoy working on automotive projects in my garage. Before you leave please take a look at my resume!
